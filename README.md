# Computational-Universe-Articles-Backup-From-2025-to-2026-08

# This collection brings together my essays on computational cosmology written between 2025 and 2026. The central focus is on a novel mechanism for mass-generating gravity, alongside the introduction of a new algebraic definition of entropy and a proposed theory of time. The discussions also touch upon the nature of dark matter and the stabilization mechanisms underlying mass, among other related topics.
